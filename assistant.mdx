The Assistant is an AI inside the app that answers questions about your community and makes changes to your project for you. Open it from **Assistant** at the top of the sidebar.

<Frame>
  ![Assistant page with the message box, Ask first and Auto modes, starter prompts and recent changes](/images/app/assistant.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  The Assistant is in beta and can make mistakes. Keep **Ask first** on to review every change before it applies, and double-check its answers.
</Warning>

## Ask or tell it what to change

Type in the box, for example a question, a request or an update you want reflected in the bot. It can only do what your role in the project allows.

- Press **Enter** to send and **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
- You can send one message every 30 seconds.

### Starter prompts

Below the box, starter prompts are grouped into four tabs: **Understand**, **Protect**, **Write** and **Set up**. Click one to send it. For example, under **Understand**:

- "Summarize this week"
- "Why was the last member banned or muted?"
- "Who are my most active members?"
- "How is the mood in the chat?"

## Ask first or Auto

Choose how the Assistant applies changes, next to the message box:

- **Ask first**: each change waits for your approval before it applies. Recommended.
- **Auto**: changes apply as soon as the Assistant makes them.

## Recent changes and history

**Recent changes**, on the right, lists every change the Assistant made in this project, from any conversation. Click **Audit Log** to see them with everything else in the [Audit Log](/audit-log).

Messages the Assistant posts as the bot are marked **Sent via the assistant** in [Chat History](/chat-history).

Open **History** at the top to go back to an earlier conversation.
