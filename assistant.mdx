The Assistant is an AI inside the app that answers questions about collony.ai and makes changes to your project for you. Open it from **Assistant** at the top of the sidebar, or press **Cmd K** (Ctrl K on Windows) and use **Ask collony**.

<Warning>
  The Assistant is in beta and can make mistakes. Keep **Ask me first** on to review every change before it applies, and double-check its answers.
</Warning>

## What you can ask

Ask how anything in collony.ai works, or tell it what to change. It can only do what your role in the project allows.

Starter prompts on the page:

| Prompt | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Optimize the FAQ** | Removes expired info and duplicates and tightens long answers. A leaner FAQ makes the bot answer faster. |
| **Paste your website or whitepaper** | Drafts your Project About and Knowledge Base, sized to keep replies cheap. |
| **Paste an update** | For example "Launch moved to March 14". It edits only the lines the update affects. |
| **Get ready for a token launch** | Stricter link rules, Surge Guard and impersonation checks. |
| **Explain a page** | For example "What does Surge Guard do?" or "Why is the bot quiet at night?" |

## Changes

Choose how the Assistant applies changes:

- **Ask me first**: each change waits for your approval. Use **Apply all** or **Discard all** to handle them together.
- **Apply automatically**: changes apply as soon as the Assistant makes them.

**Recent changes** lists every change the Assistant made in this project, from any conversation. Each change is also recorded in the [Audit Log](/audit-log).

Press Enter to send a message and Shift+Enter for a new line. Click **New chat** to start over.
