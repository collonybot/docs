Insights shows how your community talks, reacts and feels over the range you pick. It replaces the old Sentiment and Activity pages.

<Note>
  Insights is part of the Grow and Scale plans. See [Billing](/billing).
</Note>

## Summary strip

The top of the page shows the headline numbers for the selected range, each with the change against the previous period:

- **Member messages**
- **Net mood**, positive share minus negative share
- **Reactions**
- **Talking**, the number of members who posted
- **Removed**, messages the bot or your team took down

Next to it: the positive, neutral and negative split and how many answers the bot gave.

## Time range

Every tab uses the same range: 24h, 7d, 30d, 90d, or **Custom** with your own From and To dates.

## Tabs

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Overview">
    How busy the community is.

    - **Activity timeline**: messages over time in 5-minute buckets, with zoom levels from 1h to 30d. Drag across the chart to zoom in. Shaded bands mark [Surge Guard](/surge-guard) slow-mode periods.
    - **Daily active members**: members who sent a message and members who only reacted, per day.
    - **Questions and the assistant**: how many questions were asked, the share answered by the bot, by members or left unanswered, and the median time to an answer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Members">
    Who drives the conversation: the most active members and the members whose messages get the most reactions. Click a member to open their [profile](/member-details).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reactions">
    The messages the community loved most, with who reacted and when, and the emojis members use most.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sentiment">
    The mood of the chat, scored message by message.

    - Positive, neutral and negative share with message counts, and the net mood
    - **Mood over time**
    - **Most positive members** and **Most negative members**, with average score and message count

    <Tip>
      Persistent negativity from the same accounts often comes before trouble. Check those members before it turns into an incident.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rhythm">
    When the community is active, in UTC.

    - **Last 13 weeks**: one square per day. Brighter means busier, red marks a rough day.
    - Highlights: busiest day, longest streak of days with messages, roughest day and busiest weekday
    - **By weekday** and **By hour**: messages and reactions
    - A weekday by hour heatmap

    Use it to time announcements and AMAs, and to set your [Schedule](/schedule).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Messages">
    The scored messages behind the numbers, so you can read what members actually said.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
