Insights shows how your community felt and talked over the range you pick. It has two views, **Simple** and **Advanced**. Switch between them at the top of the page.

Pick the range at the top: 24h, 7d, 30d, or **More** for presets like last 14 days, this month, or a custom From and To.

## Simple view

A one-page read of the community, in cards:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Mood** | Mood score from -100 to +100, a one-line read such as "Mostly positive", the change against the previous period and the positive, neutral and negative split |
| **Messages** | Member messages, the change against the previous period and a bar per day |
| **Talking** | How many members spoke up out of everyone in the chat, split into regulars, members who came back and first-timers |
| **Questions** | The share of questions collony AI answered and how fast |
| **Best time to post** | The busiest hour of the period, in UTC |
| **Most loved message** | The message with the most reactions, and who reacted |
| **Favorite emoji** | The most used emoji and the runners-up |
| **Streak** | Days in a row with messages |
| **People of the week** | The most active member, the brightest voice and one to watch (lowest mood) |

### What they talk about

- **Topics**: the share of messages by topic, such as Social, Technical, Question, Swearing and Sarcastic, and which topic moved most
- **Left unanswered**: recent questions nobody answered. Click **Improve answers** to add them to your knowledge base.
- **Busiest channels**: the share of messages per channel

<Tip>
  Questions that keep showing up under **Left unanswered** are the fastest win. Add the answers to your [Knowledge Base](/bot-setup#knowledge-base) and the bot handles them next time.
</Tip>

### Growth

New members who joined, and where members are, split by Telegram and Discord with each platform's share of messages.

## Advanced view

The top of the Advanced view shows mood per day, member messages, reactions, bot answers, removed messages and how many members talked or only reacted. Below it are six tabs.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Overview">
    How busy the community is.

    - **Activity timeline**: messages over time, live, with zoom levels from 1h to 30d. Drag across the chart to zoom in. Shaded bands mark [Surge Guard](/surge-guard) slow-mode periods.
    - **Daily active members**: members who sent a message and members who only reacted, per day
    - **Questions and the assistant**: the share of questions answered by collony AI, by members or left unanswered, the median answer time and answer time per day
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Members">
    Who talks the most, whose messages get reactions and who reacts.

    - **Engagement**: a funnel from members in the chat to those who reacted, talked, got reactions and were loved (5 or more reactions), against the previous period
    - **Member map**: each member placed by messages and mood, sized by reactions received
    - **Most active**, **Most reacted-on** and **Top reacters** rankings

    Click a member to open their [profile](/member-details).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reactions">
    The messages the community loved most, with who reacted and when, and the emojis members use most.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sentiment">
    The mood of the chat, scored message by message.

    - Positive, neutral and negative share with message counts, and the net mood
    - **Mood over time**, with the range of net mood and the busiest day
    - **Most positive members** and **Most negative members**, with message count and average score

    <Tip>
      Persistent negativity from the same accounts often comes before trouble. Check those members before it turns into an incident.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rhythm">
    When the community is active, in UTC: a calendar of recent weeks, the busiest day and weekday, the longest streak, activity by weekday and by hour, and a weekday by hour heatmap.

    Use it to time announcements and AMAs, and to set your [Schedule](/schedule).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Messages">
    The scored messages behind the numbers, so you can read what members actually said.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
