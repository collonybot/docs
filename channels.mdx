The Channels page lists every channel the bot can see in the current project, grouped by platform. For each one you choose whether the bot is on and what role the channel plays.

The bot does not read, moderate or reply in channels that are off. It stays in the group or server but does nothing there.

</Frame>

## Channel roles

| Role | Protects | Replies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Community chat** (default): where members talk. The bot protects the chat and answers questions. | Yes | Yes |
| **Security only**: the bot removes spam and scams, mutes and bans, and never replies. | Yes | No |
| **Chat & support only**: the bot answers questions and chats. It does not moderate here. | No | Yes |
| **Moderator notifications**: a private channel for your mods. The bot posts reports of what it deleted, muted or banned. | No | Posts reports |

Click the role next to a channel to change it. In channels where the bot replies, replies also follow your [Schedule](/schedule) and the chat switches in [Settings](/settings#assistant).

<Tip>
  Set announcement and off-topic channels to **Security only** so the bot keeps them clean without joining the conversation. Create a private channel for your team and set it to **Moderator notifications** to follow what the bot does in real time.
</Tip>

## Turning channels on and off

Switch a channel on or off from its row. To change several channels at once, select them and use **Set role…**, **Turn on** or **Turn off**.

## New channels

The **New channels** settings control channels the bot picks up later, for example a new Discord channel:

- **Turn on the bot in new channels**: with this off, new channels wait on this page until you switch them on.
- **Role for new channels**: the role new channels get, Community chat by default.

## Filters

Filter the list by **Role** and **Bot access** (Full access, Limited, No access, Not checked yet). Each row also shows the channel type and when it was last active.

<Warning>
  A channel with **Limited** or **No access** is missing permissions the bot needs. Moderation can fail there until you fix the bot's permissions on the platform.
</Warning>
