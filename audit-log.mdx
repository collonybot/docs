The Audit Log records every change made in the project and every manual moderation action, with the person who made it. Changes made by the [Assistant](/assistant) and the [MCP server](/mcp) show here too.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | All, Billing, Channels, Content, Moderation, Roles, Settings |
| **Actor** | Everyone who made a change in the project |
| **From** and **To** | A date range |
| **Bot actions** | Include actions the bot took on its own |

Click **Apply** to run the filters.

## Entries

Each row shows the time, the actor, the category, the action, the target (a user, channel or content item), the details and whether it succeeded. Click a row to expand it.

Examples of what is logged:

- Roles added or removed, including batch changes from the Members page
- Channels turned on or off and channel role changes
- Content saved in [Bot Setup](/bot-setup), with the version number and a diff of what changed
- Bans, mutes and other actions taken from the app
- Settings changes, such as dismissing or restoring the setup guide

<Tip>
  If the bot starts behaving differently, filter by **Content** or **Settings** first. A recent edit to the Agent Personality or Knowledge Base is the usual cause.
</Tip>
