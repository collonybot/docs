The Audit Log records every change in the project: by your team, by the bot and by [AI apps](/ai-apps). Use it to see who changed what and when.

<Frame>
  ![Audit Log with filters and entries from the team, the bot and AI apps](/images/app/audit-log.png)
</Frame>

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Include bot actions** | Show what the bot did on its own, such as Surge Guard turning on |
| **Include AI app reads** | Show what AI apps read, not only what they changed |
| **Category** | All, Billing, Content, Moderation, Read, Roles, Schedule, Settings |
| **Actor** | Everyone who made a change, including the collony.ai team |
| **Via** | Any, or AI apps only |
| **When** | All, 24h, 7d, 30d, or **More** for presets or a custom From and To |
| **Sort** | Newest first or oldest first |

## Entries

Each entry shows the time, who did it, the action, the target (a user, channel, setting or content item), the details and whether it succeeded. Click an entry to see everything it did, including the error if it failed.

Changes made through an AI app are marked **via** the app, for example "via Claude (AI app)". Click **Undo this change** to revert one.

Examples of what is logged:

- Roles added or removed
- Schedule, settings and protection changes
- Content saved in [Bot Setup](/bot-setup)
- Mutes, bans and other moderation actions
- Surge Guard turning on or changing, by the bot

<Tip>
  If the bot starts behaving differently, filter by **Content** or **Settings** first. A recent edit to the Agent Personality or Knowledge Base is the usual cause.
</Tip>
