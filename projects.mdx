---

A **project** is a community you want collony.ai to protect. It can hold channels from Telegram and Discord, and it has its own settings, rules, analytics and plan.

The Projects page, **Your communities**, lists your projects with their status, plan, your role and when they were last active.

## Add the bot to Telegram

1. Click **\+ Add bot**, then **Add to Telegram**.
2. Telegram opens its chat picker. Pick your group and confirm the admin rights: **delete messages, restrict members, pin messages**.
3. Finish setup from the list on the Projects page.

<Warning>
  collony.ai needs the delete messages, restrict members and pin messages rights. Without them, moderation actions fail.
</Warning>

## Add the bot to Discord

1. Click **\+ Add bot**, then **Add to Discord**.
2. Authorize the bot on your server and confirm the permissions.
3. Finish setup from the list on the Projects page.

Then set what the bot does in each channel on the [Channels](/channels) page.

## Plans

Every new project starts on the Free plan. To start a 7-day trial of Protect or Grow, or to change plan, open [Billing](/billing) from the sidebar.

## Deactivated projects

Projects you deactivate from [Settings](/settings#danger-zone) move to **Deactivated projects** at the bottom of the page. A deactivated project is suspended: the bot is off, billing is canceled and it is hidden from members. Click **Reactivate** to bring it back with its settings and history.

## Feedback

Click **Feedback** in the app to send the team an idea, a problem or a question. The team reads every one. You can also chat with the team on [Telegram](https://t.me/collony_official).
