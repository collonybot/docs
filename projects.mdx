A **project** is a community you want collony.ai to protect. It can hold channels from Telegram and Discord, and it has its own settings, rules, analytics and plan.

<Frame>
  ![Projects page with community cards and filters](/images/app/projects.png)
</Frame>

## Your communities

The Projects page lists your communities as cards. Each card shows your role, the status, members, channels, cases waiting for review, when it was last active and its plan. Click **Open** to go to the project.

Filter by **All**, **Active** or **Needs attention**. A project needs attention when it has cases to review or something to fix, such as a trial about to end.

If you manage Discord servers where the bot is already installed, click **Link Discord** to see them here.

## Add the bot to Telegram

1. Click **\+ Add bot**, then **Add to Telegram**.
2. Telegram opens its chat picker. Pick your group and confirm the admin rights: **delete messages, restrict members, pin messages**.
3. Finish setup from the Projects page.

<Warning>
  collony.ai needs the delete messages, restrict members and pin messages rights. Without them, moderation actions fail.
</Warning>

## Add the bot to Discord

1. Click **\+ Add bot**, then **Add to Discord**.
2. Authorize the bot on your server and confirm the permissions.
3. Finish setup from the Projects page.

Then choose what the bot does in each channel on the [Channels](/channels) page.

## Plans

To start a 7-day trial of Protect or Grow, or to change plan, open [Billing](/billing). A project on a trial shows how much time is left on its card.

## Deactivated projects

Projects you deactivate from [Settings](/settings#deactivating-a-project) are suspended: the bot is off, billing is canceled and the project is hidden from members. Reactivate one to bring it back with its settings and history.

## Feedback

Click **Feedback** in the app to send the team an idea, a problem or a question. You can also chat with the team on [Telegram](https://t.me/collony_official).
