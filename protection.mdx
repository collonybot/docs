The Protection page controls what the bot stops on its own. Open it from **Protection** in the sidebar. It has two views, **Simple** and **Advanced**. Switch between them at the top of the page.

<Frame>
  ![Protection page in Simple view with the How strict levels, last 7 days and the protection layers](/images/app/protection.png)
</Frame>

## Simple view

### How strict

One choice sets enforcement, impersonation, Surge Guard, media checks and cleanup together. Your AI rules and blocklists stay as you set them.

| Level | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Relaxed** | Asks you before any ban. Short mutes, fewer checks. |
| **Balanced** (recommended) | Deletes, mutes and bans repeat offenders on its own. Right for most communities. |
| **Strict** | Bans on the 2nd mute, every check on, Surge Guard on. For big or often-raided chats. |

### Last 7 days

How many messages the bot removed for you, how many it sent to **Needs review**, and how many you marked as a mistake. The bar below splits them into banned, muted, deleted and review.

### Quick settings

- **Let the bot ban on its own**: on, bans happen right away and you can undo any of them from [Incidents](/incidents). Off, the bot suggests the ban and waits for you.
- **Mute length**: used for every mute, and while a ban suggestion waits. 1 hour, 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days.
- **Repeat offenders**: when to ban, for example the 3rd mute in 30 days, or never. Forgiven incidents do not count.

### Layers

Below the quick settings, each protection layer is a row you can open. Each shows how many messages it caught in the last 7 days.

| Layer | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Scam and spam rules** | The AI reads every message for these patterns, plus your own community rules. See [AI rules](/ai-rules). |
| **Blocklists** | Words and links handled instantly, no AI involved. See [Blocklists](/blocklists). |
| **Impersonation** | Catches accounts copying your team's names or pictures. See [Impersonation](/impersonation). |
| **Surge Guard** | Automatic slow mode during floods and raids. See [Surge Guard](/surge-guard). |

Open a row to change its main settings. **More in Advanced** takes you to the full settings.

## Advanced view

The Advanced view shows every setting and the full numbers.

### How a message is checked

Every message passes the layers in this order. The first layer that acts on a message decides what happens to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Surge Guard">
    Watches the message rate in each channel and turns on slow mode during a flood or raid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Impersonation protection">
    Catches members copying the name, @handle or picture of someone on your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Blocklists">
    Words, phrases and domains, handled instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI rules">
    Everything the earlier layers did not catch is read by the AI, using your community rules and the rule presets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Activity

For the range you pick (24h, 7d, 30d, or **More** for presets like last 14 days, this month, or a custom From and To):

- Actions and incidents, with the change against the previous period
- Deleted, muted and banned counts
- **Actions per day**, with the busiest day and which layer did most of the work
- **By layer**: Surge and flood, Impersonation, Blocklists, AI rules and Filters, each with its deletions, mutes and bans
