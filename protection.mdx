The Protection page controls what collony.ai removes or restricts in your community. Open it from **Protection** in the sidebar.

## How a message is checked

Every message passes through the layers in this order. The first layer that acts on a message decides what happens to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Surge Guard">
    Watches the message rate in each channel and turns on slow mode during a flood or raid. See [Surge Guard](/surge-guard).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Impersonation protection">
    Catches accounts that copy the name or look of your admins and moderators. It relies on your team having the right roles in [Members](/members).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Blocklists">
    Words, phrases and domains you or collony.ai have blocked. Handled instantly, with no AI involved. See [Blocklists](/blocklists).
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI rules">
    Everything the earlier layers did not catch is read by the AI, using your community rules and the rule presets. See [AI rules](/ai-rules).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The AI layer is part of the Protect, Grow and Scale plans. On the Free plan, Surge Guard, blocklists and the link and scam source checks still run. See [Billing](/billing).
</Note>

## Activity summary

The top of the page shows what Protection did in the selected range (24h, 7d or 30d):

- **Actions and incidents**, with the change against the previous period
- **Deleted, muted and banned** counts
- A chart of incidents and actions per day

## By layer

The **By layer** panel splits the same numbers by the layer that acted: Surge and flood, Impersonation, Blocklists, AI rules and Filters. Each row shows how many messages were deleted and how many members were muted or banned. Click **Incidents** to see the individual cases.

Use it to spot which layer is doing the work. If AI rules carry almost everything, adding the most common scam domains to a blocklist makes those removals instant.
