collony.ai watches your Telegram and Discord communities around the clock. It removes spam and scams in real time, answers member questions from your knowledge base, and shows you trends your team would otherwise miss.

The app at [app.collony.ai](https://app.collony.ai) is where you set up the bot, review every moderation action, track community mood and manage your team, across all your projects and both platforms.

<Note>
  Need help or want to see collony.ai in action? Join the community on [Telegram](https://t.me/collony_official) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/na6xcu7xV7).
</Note>
