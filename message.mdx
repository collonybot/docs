Click **Open message** on any message in [Chat History](/chat-history), [Incidents](/incidents), the Overview or a member profile to open its detail page. Click **Back to Chat History** to return.

## Message

The sender, the time and the message itself, including what it replied to. If a person on your team posted it as the bot, it shows who and how, for example **Sent via the assistant**.

- **Translate** shows the message in your language.
- **Open in Telegram** (or Discord) jumps to the message on the platform.

Below it: the platform, message ID, reactions and, for bot messages, who sent it and when it was scheduled.

## Actions

The **Actions** menu deletes the message, mutes the sender for 24 hours (default), 1 hour or 7 days, bans them or opens their profile.

If the message led to a moderation case, the incident shows here too. Click **Open incident** for the full case.

## Author

The sender's join date, last activity, status, message count, mood, share of deleted messages, incidents and latest messages. Click **Open profile** for their [full profile](/member-details).

## collony AI analysis

The scores collony.ai gave the message:

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Positivity** | How positive or negative the message is |
| **Maliciousness** | How likely the message is a scam or attack |
| **Sarcasm**, **Shilling**, **FUD**, **Inside joke** | Signals the AI picked up |

Labels such as Question, Explanation, Technical, Social, Has meme and Contains swearing show the topic of the message.

## Chat context

The messages before and after this one, including bot replies, each with its mood score. Click **View full chat history** to see more. Use it to judge a message in context before you act.
