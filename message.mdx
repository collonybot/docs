Click any message in [Chat History](/chat-history), [Incidents](/incidents) or a member profile to open its detail page. Click **Back to Chat History** to return.

## Message header

Shows the sender, the time, the message itself and its status, for example **Message deleted**. Two buttons sit above it:

- **Translate** shows the message in your language.
- **Open in Telegram** (or Discord) jumps to the message on the platform.

## Incident

If the message led to a moderation action, the page shows the incident: its severity, the actions taken (deleted, muted, ban suggested, unmuted) and who took them, the bot or a moderator. Click **Open incident** for the full case.

## Sentiment analysis

The scores collony.ai gave the message:

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Positivity** | How positive or negative the message is |
| **Maliciousness** | How likely the message is a scam or attack |
| **Sarcasm**, **Shilling**, **FUD**, **Inside Joke** | Signals the AI picked up |
| **Reactions** | Reactions the message received |

Labels such as Question, Explanation, Technical, Social, Has Meme and Contains Swearing show the topic of the message.

## Chat context

The messages before and after this one, including bot replies and forwarded posts, each with its own labels and positivity score. Expand any message to see its details. Use it to judge a message in the context of the conversation before you act.
