Blocklists handle words, phrases and links instantly, before the AI reads a message. They run on every plan, including Free. Open them from the **Blocklists** tab on the [Protection](/protection) page.

## What a match does

For each list you pick what happens when a message matches: delete, mute, ban or flag.

- **Flag** adds the message to **Needs review** on the [Incidents](/incidents) page.
- **Flag only** leaves the message up and lets the AI check it as well.

## Keep collony.ai's lists up to date

collony.ai maintains shared lists of scam words and domains. With this switch on, words and domains collony.ai adds or retires apply to your project automatically. With it off, each update waits on this page until you review it.

## Blocked domains

Links to these domains and all their subdomains are handled instantly. Add the domains you see scammers post most often.

## Allowed domains

Domains on this list are never blocked: your own site, your docs, your partners. An allowed domain wins over every blocked domain and over the [new member link limit](/enforcement#new-members).

<Tip>
  Add your official links to Allowed domains before you tighten link rules. It keeps the bot from removing your own announcements.
</Tip>
