The Schedule page sets what the bot does, and when. Use it to moderate around the clock while limiting chat replies to the hours your community is most active.

<Frame>
  ![Schedule page with the schedule options, timezone and weekly grid](/images/app/schedule.png)
</Frame>

## Bot enabled

The master switch. When it is off, the bot does nothing, whatever the schedule says.

## Choose a schedule

| Option | What the bot does |
| --- | --- |
| **Always Security** | Moderates every hour of every day, with no chat replies |
| **Always Chat + Security** | Moderates and replies in chat, 24/7 |
| **Custom** | You pick the hours and the mode for each |

The page shows the mode right now and when the next change happens.

## Timezone

Times are shown in the schedule timezone. Pick it from the list, from UTC-10 (Honolulu) to UTC+12 (Auckland), so the grid matches your community's day.

## Custom schedule

Pick a mode to paint with, then paint the weekly grid:

| Mode | What the bot does |
| --- | --- |
| **Security** | Moderates, but does not reply or join the conversation |
| **Chat + Security** | Moderates and replies: chat, FAQ answers and reactions |
| **Off** | Does nothing in that slot |

1. Drag across the grid to paint 30-minute slots.
2. Click a day name to fill that day, or an hour label to fill that hour on every day.
3. Click **Save changes**, or **Discard** to drop them.

<Tip>
  A common setup: Chat \+ Security during your community's busy hours and Security the rest of the time. Scams are still removed 24/7, and the bot only chats when people are around.
</Tip>
