title: "Login"
description: "Sign in to collony.ai with Discord, Google or Telegram and link your accounts so you can manage your communities."
---

Go to [app.collony.ai](https://app.collony.ai/login) and sign in with one of three options:

- **Continue with Discord**
- **Continue with Google**
- **Continue with Telegram**

Any option works, whichever platforms you moderate. If you used collony.ai before, sign in with the same option you used then.

You can switch the app language from the language menu: English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Ukrainian, Chinese (Traditional and Simplified) or Russian.

## Link your accounts

Link Telegram, Discord and Google to one account so all your communities show up in one place. Click your name in the top right corner to open your **Account** page. Under **Linked accounts**, connect a platform to sign in with it and manage the projects you run there.

The Account page also lists the [AI apps](/mcp) you connected, with their access, projects and expiry.

### Link Telegram

Click **Link with Telegram**.

<Warning>
  If Telegram signs you in but the Telegram app does not keep the session, open collony.ai in your browser to continue. If linking does not work because Telegram is not installed on your computer, link manually instead.
</Warning>

To link manually:

1. Open [@CollonyBot](https://t.me/CollonyBot) on Telegram.
2. Send `/linkweb`.
3. Click the link the bot replies with. It expires after 5 minutes.

Once linked, your Telegram groups appear on the Projects page and in the project switcher.

### Link Discord

Sign in with Discord, or click **Link with Discord** on your account page. You can then claim and manage the servers where the bot is installed.
