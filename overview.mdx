Overview is the first page you see when you open a project. It shows the project's health at a glance and what needs your attention.

<Frame>
  ![Overview page with headline numbers, Needs you, recent incidents and topics](/images/app/overview.png)
</Frame>

The header shows the project name and status, its platforms, channel count, current bot mode and your role. Pick the range at the top right: 24h, 7d, 30d, or **More** for presets like last 14 days, this month, or a custom From and To. Click **Customize** to choose which sections you see.

## Headline numbers

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | Members in the community, how many joined and how many are talking |
| **Incidents handled** | Moderation cases, and how fast the bot took its first action on average |
| **Bot replies** | Replies the bot sent, as a share of all messages |
| **Mood** | Net mood from -100 to +100, with the positive, neutral and negative split |

## Needs you

The things waiting on you, for example an incident to review, a ban the bot suggested, Surge Guard turning on or the Instant guard muting a flooder. Each has a **Review** or **Open** button.

**Everything else** confirms what is working, such as connected platforms, protected channels and credit usage.

## Recent incidents

The latest moderation cases, with the member, channel, severity, what caught it (collony AI, Surge Guard, Instant guard, Blocklist) and what was done. Open the menu on any incident to open the incident, message or member, mute, ban, delete, mark reviewed, forgive or mark it as a mistake. Click **View all** to go to [Incidents](/incidents).

## The bot

Whether the bot is on, its current mode (for example Security only), the active hours and the next mode change. Click **Schedule** to change it.

**Quick settings** has the switches you change most: Basic chat, FAQ answers, Emoji reactions and **Let the bot ban on its own**.

## What they talk about

The share of messages by topic, such as Social, Technical, Question, Swearing and Sarcastic, and which topic moved most. Click **Insights** for more.

## People of the week

The most loved message, the most active member, the most positive member and the most negative member. Click **Members** for the full list.

## Recent chat

A live feed of the latest messages with their mood score. Open the menu on a message to open it, open it on Telegram or Discord, copy its link or text, view the member, mute, ban or delete. Click **View all** to go to [Chat History](/chat-history).

## Top offenders

The members with the most incidents, with quick actions: open profile, mute, ban or whitelist.
