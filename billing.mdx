Each project has its own subscription. Paid plans are billed monthly or yearly. Usage is measured in daily credits that reset at 00:00 UTC.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Billed yearly | Platforms | Daily usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | \$0 | \$0 | Telegram or Discord | Up to 1,000 messages, no AI |
| **Protect** | \$29 / mo | \$24.17 / mo | Telegram or Discord | 500 security credits |
| **Grow** | \$99 / mo | \$82.50 / mo | Telegram and Discord | 2,000 security + 2,000 chat credits |
| **Scale** | Custom | Custom | Telegram and Discord | Sized to your community |

Yearly billing gives you 2 months free.

## Free trial

Protect and Grow come with a 7-day free trial, no credit card required. The trial includes everything in the plan you picked. If you do not subscribe when the trial ends, the project moves to the Free plan. Your config, rules and history are kept.

<Tip>
  Use the trial to dial in your rules and knowledge base before you commit. The bot needs a few days of real traffic to surface false positives worth fixing.
</Tip>

## Credits

- **Security credits**: one credit per message checked.
- **Chat credits**: one credit per AI reply the bot sends.

Credits reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Today's usage is shown at the top of the Billing page.

When credits run out, protection never stops. AI chat replies pause first and you get a notice, so scams and spam keep getting removed until the next reset.

## Subscribing

Open **Billing** from the sidebar, select a plan, and choose monthly or yearly. Payment is handled by Stripe's secure checkout. collony.ai never stores your card details.

## Changing or canceling plans

Switch plans from the Billing page at any time. The difference is prorated, so you only pay for what you use. If you cancel, your paid features stay on until the end of the current billing period and the project then moves to the Free plan. Your project data stays intact, so you can resubscribe anytime.
